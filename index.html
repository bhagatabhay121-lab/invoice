Privacy Policy

Free Invoice Generator (package name: com.abhay.invoice)
Effective date: 30 September 2026 · Last updated: 30 September 2026

Free Invoice Generator ("the app", "we", "us") helps you create GST invoices, keep a list of customers and products, and export invoices as PDF files. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and honest.

1. The short version

  • Your invoices, customers, products, business profile, logo, signature and bank details are stored only on your phone. We do not upload them and we cannot see them.
  • We collect a small amount of usage information (your name, when you open the app and how long you use it) to understand how the app is used and to improve it.
  • When you look up a GSTIN, the GSTIN and the CAPTCHA you type are sent to a GST portal service to fetch the business's public details.
  • We do not show ads, do not sell your data and do not share it for marketing.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following is created by you and saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to any server:

  • Your business profile: business name, address, GSTIN, PAN, phone, email, bank details, UPI ID, terms text, logo, signature and watermark
  • Customers, products and invoices you create, including invoice numbers, items, amounts and notes
  • App settings such as language, theme, default GST rate and Do Not Disturb preference

Because this information is kept only on your phone, we do not hold a backup of it. If you uninstall the app, clear its data or lose your phone, this information is erased and we cannot recover it. The app has a Backup screen that copies your data as text so that you can save it yourself (for example in Notes, email or WhatsApp). What you do with that copied text is up to you.

3. Information we collect (usage information)

When you first open the app you are asked for your name. From then on the app sends the following to our Firebase Realtime Database, provided by Google:

InformationWhy we collect it
The name you enterTo tell testers and users apart when we review usage and feedback
A random install ID generated by the app (it is not your device advertising ID, phone number or email)To count how many people use the app without counting the same person twice
Install date, last-seen time, number of visits and total time the app was open, per day and in totalTo measure daily and total users and how much the app is used
App version, phone platform (for example Android) and selected app languageTo fix problems that affect particular versions or languages

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, advertising ID, or the contents of your invoices, customers or products.

4. GSTIN lookup

If you type a GSTIN when adding a customer, the app can fetch that business's public registration details (such as trade name, address and status). To do this, the GSTIN and the CAPTCHA text you type are sent over the internet to a GST portal service (a public GST search service). The details returned are put into the customer form on your phone. This lookup only happens when you enter a GSTIN and choose to complete the CAPTCHA, and you can skip it and fill in the details manually. That service has its own privacy practices, which we do not control.

5. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Photos / files: only when you choose to add a logo, signature or watermark image. The image you pick is stored on your phone inside the app.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): only if you turn on the "Do Not Disturb while the app is open" setting. It is used to switch Do Not Disturb on while the app is open and off when you leave. Nothing about this is sent to us.
  • Internet: to upload the usage information in section 3 and to do the GSTIN lookup in section 4.

6. Sharing and printing invoices

When you export, print or share an invoice as a PDF, the file is created on your phone and handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, email or a printer). Once you share it, it is handled by that app and the person you send it to, under their rules. We do not receive a copy.

7. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your information. The usage information in section 3 is stored with Google Firebase, which acts as our service provider (see Firebase privacy information). The GSTIN lookup in section 4 goes to the GST portal service. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

8. How long we keep information

Information on your phone stays until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Usage information in our database is kept while the app is in use and for as long as we need it to understand and improve the app, after which it may be deleted or anonymised. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see section 10).

9. Security

Information sent to Firebase and to the GST service travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Data stored on your phone is protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock, so we recommend keeping one. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction or deletion: email us at the address below with the name you entered in the app and we will look up and delete or correct the usage information linked to it. Please mention your app version if you can.
  • Delete data on your phone: uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's Settings.
  • Stop usage information: uninstalling the app stops all uploads.

Because the install ID is random and not linked to your phone number or email, we may need the name you entered to find your record.

11. Children's privacy

The app is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 18 where the law of your country requires parental consent). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us a name, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, show a notice in the app. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
